
### 🩺 Dozify — Smart AI-Powered Pre-Consultation System  
**MERN • AI • Cloud • Healthcare**

Dozify is an AI-driven healthcare platform designed to optimize the **pre-consultation workflow** in hospitals and clinics, especially where doctors face heavy patient loads and language barriers.

---

## 🚨 Problem

- Doctors often cannot consult all patients effectively due to time constraints and long queues.
- Language barriers between patients and doctors cause miscommunication.
- Rural doctors struggle to analyze complex medical reports.
- Patients frequently lose prescription history, leading to repeated or incorrect treatments.

---

## 💡 Solution

Dozify enables patients to submit symptoms **before the appointment** using voice in their regional language and upload medical reports.

Doctors receive:
- Patient symptom **audio**
- AI-generated **transcripts**
- **Language translation** options for better understanding
- **AI-analyzed medical reports**
- Access to **next patient details in advance**, reducing consultation time

This allows doctors to consult patients faster, with better preparation and confidence.

---

## 🔑 Key Capabilities

- Voice-based symptom capture (regional languages)
- Multilingual speech-to-text & translation
- AI-driven medical report analysis
- Doctor dashboard with patient queue visibility
- Secure storage of medical records and prescriptions

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React, Tailwind CSS  
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, Cloudflare Workers  
- **Database:** MongoDB  
- **AI:** OpenAI Whisper, LLaMA 3.1  
- **Cloud:** AWS S3, Textract, IAM  

---

## 🏗️ System Highlights

- OpenAI Whisper processes patient audio into multilingual transcripts
- AI models summarize symptoms and analyze reports automatically
- AWS S3 securely stores patient data with MongoDB references
- Cloudflare Workers enable low-latency, scalable API handling

---

## 📌 Impact

- Reduced doctor consultation time
- Improved accuracy in patient understanding
- Enabled rural doctors to analyze reports confidently
- Preserved patient medical history digitally
